| **nsclc_pt01_egfr_l858r_t790m_osimertinib_resistant** | Lung Adenocarcinoma (Never/Light Smoker) (EGFR L858R primary mutation + T790M gatekeeper, secondary acquired C797S mutation) | Osimertinib (3rd-generation covalent EGFR TKI) | **Rapid secondary progression at 4.1 months, diffuse bilateral pulmonary lymphangitic spread, death at 11.2 months** | C797S mutation abolishes covalent binding of Osimertinib, maintaining uninterrupted downstream MAPK and PI3K signaling |
| **nsclc_pt02_kras_g12c_sotorasib_resistant** | KRAS-Mutant / STK11-KEAP1 Co-Mutant (SAK Subgroup) (KRAS G12C mutation, STK11/LKB1 biallelic loss, KEAP1 null mutation) | Sotorasib (AMG 510 covalent KRAS-G12C inhibitor) | **Primary resistance with disease progression at 2.6 months, multiple brain metastases, death at 6.8 months** | STK11 loss hyperactivates mTORC1 while KEAP1 null stabilizes NRF2, driving high antioxidant flux and bypassing KRAS dependency |
| **nsclc_pt03_alk_eml4_fusion_lorlatinib_resistant** | Never-Smoker Young Adenocarcinoma (ALK-Rearranged) (EML4-ALK Variant 3 fusion + acquired solvent-front ALK G1202R mutation) | Alectinib followed by Lorlatinib (3rd-generation brain-penetrant ALK TKI) | **Leptomeningeal carcinomatosis and spinal drop metastases at 5.2 months, death at 13.5 months** | G1202R mutation introduces steric clash with Lorlatinib, maintaining persistent STAT3 and PI3K/Akt pro-survival signals |
| **nsclc_pt04_met_exon14_skipping_capmatinib_refractory** | Pleomorphic / Pulmonary Sarcomatoid Carcinoma (PSC) (MET Exon 14 skipping splice site mutation + high focal MET gene amplification (GCN >10)) | Capmatinib (selective MET inhibitor) | **Initial short stability then fulminant pleural effusion and chest wall invasion at 3.3 months, death at 6.1 months** | Secondary D1228N kinase domain mutation blocks Capmatinib, while high HGF autocrine flux hyperactivates STAT3 and MCL-1 |
| **nsclc_pt05_egfr_ex19del_acquired_met_bypass** | Asian Non-Smoker Lung Adenocarcinoma (EGFR exon 19 deletion with high focal c-MET amplification emerging under 1st-line Osimertinib) | Osimertinib monotherapy | **Progression-free survival of 6.0 months, rapid bilobar liver metastases, death at 14.0 months** | Emergence of MET amplification recruits ERBB3 and drives constitutive PI3K/Akt activation, bypassing EGFR inhibition |
| **nsclc_pt06_pdl1_high_immunotherapy_exceptional_responder** | Squamous Cell Lung Carcinoma (Former Smoker) (High Tumor Mutational Burden (TMB), PD-L1 Tumor Proportion Score (TPS) 95%, All-Wild-Type Drivers) | Pembrolizumab (anti-PD-1 checkpoint inhibitor monotherapy) | **Complete pathological and metabolic response (CR), ongoing disease-free survival >54 months (SURVIVED / Cured)** | Intact caspase and Bak/Bax machinery; absence of oncogene-driven Bcl-2/Mcl-1 locks permits complete immune eradication |
| **nsclc_pt07_squamous_pi3k_akt_sox2_amplified** | Central Bronchogenic Squamous Cell Carcinoma (PIK3CA E545K helical domain mutation, TP53 R248W mutation, high necrotic hypoxia signature) | Carboplatin + Gemcitabine + Necitumumab | **Primary chemo-refractory disease, local cavitation, mediastinal invasion and death at 4.8 months** | Constitutive PI3K/Akt signaling directly phosphorylates and inactivates BAD and Caspase-9, preventing chemotherapy-induced apoptosis |
| **nsclc_pt08_pan_refractory_kras_tp53_keap1_triplet** | Pan-Refractory Invasive Lung Adenocarcinoma (Concurrent KRAS G12V, TP53 R273H, KEAP1 null mutation, strong TGF-beta mesenchymal stroma) | Carboplatin + Pemetrexed + Pembrolizumab | **Progression-free survival of 1.9 months, extensive hepatic and adrenal metastases, death at 4.9 months** | Co-occurring KRAS, p53, and KEAP1 defects hyper-stabilize NRF2, MCL-1, and XIAP, rendering both chemo and checkpoint blockade ineffective |
