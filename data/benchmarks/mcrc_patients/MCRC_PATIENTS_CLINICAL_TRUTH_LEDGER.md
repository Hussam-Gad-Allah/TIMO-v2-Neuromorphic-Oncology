| **mcrc_pt04_msi_high_immunotherapy_exceptional_responder** | Microsatellite Instability-High (MSI-H) / Mismatch Repair Deficient (dMMR) (High mutational burden, wild-type KRAS/BRAF, intact TP53 apoptotic signaling) | Pembrolizumab (anti-PD-1 checkpoint inhibitor) | **Complete pathological and radiological response (CR), ongoing disease-free remission >48 months (SURVIVED / Cured)** | Intact intrinsic apoptotic cascade; lack of downstream anti-apoptotic Bcl-2 blocks allows rapid caspase-mediated tumor destruction |
| **mcrc_pt05_pi3k_pten_double_hit_refractory** | Chromosomal Instability (CIN) Rectal Cancer (Activating PIK3CA-E545K mutation, PTEN deletion, APC loss, TP53 mutation) | CapeOX (Capecitabine + Oxaliplatin) + Bevacizumab | **Chemo-refractory, pelvic recurrence and rapid pulmonary metastases at 4.0 months, death at 9.0 months** | Dual hit on PI3K/Akt pathway constitutively hyper-activates Akt, hyper-stabilizing XIAP and MCL-1 against oxaliplatin DNA adducts |
| **mcrc_pt06_wnt_driven_myc_hyperactivated** | FAP-Associated Aggressive Colorectal Carcinoma (Biallelic APC truncation, nuclear beta-catenin hyper-accumulation, high MYC expression, TP53 mut) | FOLFIRI + Aflibercept | **Rapid hepatic and splenic metastases at 3.8 months, refractory to second-line therapies, death at 8.2 months** | Unchecked nuclear TCF4/beta-catenin drives high CyclinD1 and MYC transcription, suppressing BAX pore formation and overriding G1 arrest |
| **mcrc_pt07_hypoxic_stem_liver_metastasis** | Metastatic Stem-Like / Mesenchymal CRC (CMS4) (KRAS G12V mutation, APC loss, high HGF and TGF-beta stromal expression, severe hypoxia) | Regorafenib (oral multikinase inhibitor salvage therapy) | **Drug-induced toxic hepatitis, progressive hepatomegaly with necrotic tumor expansion at 2.9 months, death at 5.5 months** | Hypoxic cancer stem cells activate STAT3/NF-kB, driving robust MCL-1 and c-FLIP expression to completely silence death receptor caspases |
| **mcrc_pt08_pan_refractory_triplet_mutant** | Multi-Line Resistant Metastatic Colorectal Cancer (Concurrent KRAS G12D, PIK3CA H1047R, APC loss, TP53 mut, high MET and EGFR co-expression) | Trifluridine/Tipiracil (Lonsurf) + Fruquintinib | **Progression-free survival of 1.8 months, disseminated carcinomatosis and hepatic failure, death at 4.2 months** | Simultaneous signaling across MAPK, PI3K, and Wnt pathways establishes a triple-tier anti-apoptotic lock (MCL1 + BCLxL + XIAP) |
