# Official Clinical Ledger: Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC - Recalcitrant Neuroendocrine) Patients

### sclc_pt01_classic_ascl1_bcl2_hyperdependent
- **Subtype:** SCLC-A Subtype (ASCL1-High / Classic Neuroendocrine)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Cisplatin + Etoposide chemotherapy
- **Outcome:** Brief partial response for 3.8 mo, rapid brain metastases, death at 7.5 mo
- **Resistance:** High BCL-2 transcription creates an impenetrable mitochondrial shield against platinum adducts

### sclc_pt02_slfn11_loss_acquired_chemo_refractory
- **Subtype:** SLFN11-Epigenetically Silenced Platinum-Resistant SCLC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Carboplatin + Etoposide + Atezolizumab (IMpower133)
- **Outcome:** Primary refractory, rapid progression at 2.1 mo, death at 4.2 mo
- **Resistance:** Loss of SLFN11 allows cancer cells to bypass replication fork stalling and DNA crosslinks

### sclc_pt03_myc_amplified_sclc_y_aurora_refractory
- **Subtype:** SCLC-Y / Variant Subtype with High MYC Amplification
- **Hospital Treatment:** Lurbinectedin salvage therapy
- **Outcome:** Progression at 2.4 mo, bulky mediastinal progression
- **Resistance:** MYC hyperactivates downstream MCL-1 and CDK2, driving rapid cell-cycle override

### sclc_pt04_durvalumab_exceptional_long_term_survivor
- **Subtype:** Extensive-Stage SCLC with Long-Term Immunotherapy Benefit
- **Hospital Treatment:** Platinum + Etoposide + Durvalumab (CASPIAN trial)
- **Outcome:** Durable Progression-Free Survival >32 mo (Long-Term Survivor)
- **Resistance:** High clonal neoantigen load induces durable cytotoxic T-cell memory

### sclc_pt05_neurod1_high_sclc_n_aggressive
- **Subtype:** SCLC-N Subtype (NEUROD1-Driven Primitive Small Cell)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Topotecan monotherapy
- **Outcome:** Progression at 2.8 mo, widespread hepatic metastases
- **Resistance:** NEUROD1 sustains high BCL-xL and STAT3 signaling, neutralizing topoisomerase traps

### sclc_pt06_notch_loss_neuroendocrine_hyperplastic
- **Subtype:** NOTCH-Inactivated Pure Neuroendocrine Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Tarlatamab (DLL3-targeted BiTE)
- **Outcome:** PFS 15.2 mo, significant clinical response
- **Resistance:** High surface DLL3 expression enables potent T-cell redirection

### sclc_pt07_parp_inhibitor_resistant_hr_proficient
- **Subtype:** Homologous Recombination Proficient Recurrent SCLC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Olaparib + Temozolomide
- **Outcome:** Progression at 3.0 mo, leptomeningeal spread
- **Resistance:** Rapid repair of single-strand breaks overrides PARP entrapment

### sclc_pt08_pan_refractory_quadruple_driver_lethal
- **Subtype:** Pan-Refractory Quadruple-Feature Lethal SCLC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Multi-agent salvage chemotherapy + IO
- **Outcome:** Rapid failure at 1.4 mo, death at 2.8 mo
- **Resistance:** Simultaneous loss of both tumor suppressors, MYC oncogene addiction, and SLFN11 shutdown
