# Official Clinical Ledger: Thyroid Carcinoma (THCA - Papillary, Anaplastic, Medullary) Patients

### thca_pt01_braf_v600e_radioiodine_refractory
- **Subtype:** Radioiodine-Refractory Papillary Thyroid Carcinoma (RAIR-PTC)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Lenvatinib (oral multikinase inhibitor)
- **Outcome:** PFS 18.3 mo (SELECT trial benefit)
- **Resistance:** Marked response initially; eventual resistance via PI3K/Akt feedback upregulation

### thca_pt02_ret_fusion_selpercatinib_responder
- **Subtype:** CCDC6-RET Translocated Papillary Thyroid Cancer
- **Hospital Treatment:** Selpercatinib (highly selective RET kinase inhibitor)
- **Outcome:** Complete Remission >30 mo (LIBRETTO-001 benefit)
- **Resistance:** Potent uncoupling of RET collapses downstream MAPK and PI3K pathways

### thca_pt03_anaplastic_braf_p53_double_hit_lethal
- **Subtype:** Anaplastic Thyroid Carcinoma (ATC) - Ultra-Aggressive
- **Hospital Treatment:** Dabrafenib + Trametinib salvage
- **Outcome:** Primary failure at 2.1 mo, asphyxiation, death at 3.8 mo
- **Resistance:** Co-occurrence of p53 loss and TERT drives unstoppable mitotic immortality

### thca_pt04_ras_mutant_follicular_carcinoma
- **Subtype:** NRAS Q61R Mutant Follicular Thyroid Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Sorafenib monotherapy
- **Outcome:** Progression at 4.5 mo, widespread pulmonary metastases
- **Resistance:** Feedback activation of CRAF and Akt shields cancer cells from Sorafenib

### thca_pt05_medullary_ret_m918t_cabozantinib_res
- **Subtype:** MEN2B / Sporadic Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma (MTC)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Cabozantinib (RET / VEGFR2 / MET inhibitor)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 5.2 mo, hepatic lesions, death at 11.0 mo
- **Resistance:** Acquired gatekeeper mutation impedes TKI binding pocket access

### thca_pt06_tert_promoter_aggressive_recurrent
- **Subtype:** High-Risk TERT-Mutated Tall-Cell PTC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Radioiodine ablation + Lenvatinib
- **Outcome:** Progression at 6.0 mo, invasive tracheal invasion
- **Resistance:** Telomerase immortality maintains genomic survival despite DNA radiation

### thca_pt07_pi3k_akt_hyperactive_refractory
- **Subtype:** PIK3CA-Mutant Advanced Thyroid Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Everolimus (mTOR inhibitor) + Lenvatinib
- **Outcome:** Progression at 3.4 mo, pleural effusion
- **Resistance:** mTOR inhibition triggers relief of feedback inhibition on Akt/Mcl-1

### thca_pt08_pan_refractory_anaplastic_triplet_hit
- **Subtype:** Pan-Refractory Quadruple-Feature Anaplastic Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Chemo-IO followed by multi-TKI salvage
- **Outcome:** Death at 2.0 mo, fulminant neck mass
- **Resistance:** Simultaneous activation of dual kinase drivers with p53 loss locks apoptosis
