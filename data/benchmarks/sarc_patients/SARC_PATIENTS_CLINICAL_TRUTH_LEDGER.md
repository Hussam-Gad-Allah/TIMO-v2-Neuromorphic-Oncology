# Official Clinical Ledger: Soft Tissue Sarcoma & GIST (SARC) Patients

### sarc_pt01_gist_kit_exon11_imatinib_resistant
- **Subtype:** Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumor (GIST)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Imatinib mesylate 400mg daily
- **Outcome:** Progression at 5.2 mo, multiple liver metastases
- **Resistance:** Secondary ATP activation loop mutation impairs Imatinib binding

### sarc_pt02_dedifferentiated_liposarcoma_mdm2_amp
- **Subtype:** Dedifferentiated Liposarcoma (DDLPS)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Doxorubicin monotherapy
- **Outcome:** Primary chemo-refractory at 2.4 mo, retroperitoneal mass >25cm
- **Resistance:** Massive MDM2 amplification targets p53 for constant degradation

### sarc_pt03_leiomyosarcoma_rb1_p53_double_null
- **Subtype:** Uterine / Retroperitoneal Leiomyosarcoma (LMS)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Gemcitabine + Docetaxel
- **Outcome:** Refractory at 3.0 mo, multiple pulmonary cannonball lesions
- **Resistance:** Complete ablation of cell-cycle arrest and apoptosis checkpoints

### sarc_pt04_pdgfra_d842v_avapritinib_responder
- **Subtype:** PDGFRA-Mutant Gastric GIST
- **Hospital Treatment:** Avapritinib (NAVIGATOR trial protocol)
- **Outcome:** Durable Complete Remission >36 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Selective conformation-specific uncoupling of mutant PDGFRA

### sarc_pt05_synovial_sarcoma_ss18_ssx_fusion
- **Subtype:** Monophasic Synovial Sarcoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Ifosfamide + Doxorubicin
- **Outcome:** Progression at 4.1 mo, pleural effusion
- **Resistance:** Epigenetic transcriptional rewiring sustains Mcl-1 and Bcl-2

### sarc_pt06_undifferentiated_pleomorphic_sarcoma_ups
- **Subtype:** High-Grade Undifferentiated Pleomorphic Sarcoma (UPS)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Pembrolizumab (SARC028 trial)
- **Outcome:** PFS 19.5 mo, major clinical benefit
- **Resistance:** High neoantigenic burden and inflammatory microenvironment

### sarc_pt07_angiosarcoma_radiation_induced_cmyc_amp
- **Subtype:** Post-Radiation Cutaneous Angiosarcoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Paclitaxel weekly
- **Outcome:** Progression at 2.8 mo, scalp ulceration, death at 5.5 mo
- **Resistance:** MYC overexpression overrides taxane mitotic apoptosis

### sarc_pt08_pan_refractory_kit_mdm2_p53_triplet
- **Subtype:** Pan-Refractory Triplet-Hit Sarcoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Sunitinib followed by Regorafenib salvage
- **Outcome:** Rapid failure at 1.8 mo, death at 3.4 mo
- **Resistance:** Simultaneous kinase driving with total p53 shutdown
