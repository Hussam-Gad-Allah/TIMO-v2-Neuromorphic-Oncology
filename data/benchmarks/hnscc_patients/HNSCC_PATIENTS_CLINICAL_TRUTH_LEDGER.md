# Official Clinical Ledger: Head & Neck Squamous Cell Carcinoma (HNSCC) Patients

### hnscc_pt01_egfr_amp_cetuximab_resistant
- **Subtype:** HPV-Negative EGFR-Amplified Laryngeal Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Cetuximab (anti-EGFR) + Platinum/5-FU (EXTREME)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 4.5 mo, bilateral neck nodal recurrence
- **Resistance:** Parallel PI3K/Akt activation and persistent STAT3 sustain MCL-1 despite Cetuximab

### hnscc_pt02_hpv_positive_exceptional_responder
- **Subtype:** HPV-Associated Oropharyngeal Carcinoma (p16+)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Cisplatin + Radiation therapy
- **Outcome:** Complete Remission >60 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Intact apoptotic machinery readily responds to genotoxic radiation damage

### hnscc_pt03_ccnd1_amp_cdk4_hyperactive_refractory
- **Subtype:** CCND1-Amplified Recurrent Oral Cavity Squamous Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Pembrolizumab + Chemo (KEYNOTE-048)
- **Outcome:** Early failure at 2.6 mo, carotid blowout, death at 5.1 mo
- **Resistance:** Unrestrained CDK4/6 activity overrides cell-cycle checkpoint arrest

### hnscc_pt04_pi3k_mutant_radioresistant
- **Subtype:** PIK3CA Helical Mutant Radioresistant Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Docetaxel + Cisplatin + 5-FU (TPF induction)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 3.1 mo, pulmonary metastases
- **Resistance:** Constitutive Akt activation prevents radiation-induced cytochrome c release

### hnscc_pt05_notch1_loss_stem_like_aggressive
- **Subtype:** NOTCH1-Deficient Dedifferentiated Oral Cancer
- **Hospital Treatment:** Nivolumab monotherapy
- **Outcome:** Primary immune evasion at 2.1 mo, death at 4.8 mo
- **Resistance:** Mesenchymal transition upregulates XIAP and BCL-xL survival buffer

### hnscc_pt06_pembrolizumab_cured_high_cps
- **Subtype:** PD-L1 High (CPS >= 50) Squamous Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Pembrolizumab monotherapy
- **Outcome:** Durable Complete Remission >36 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Absence of oncogene-driven anti-apoptotic traps allows cytotoxic T-cell lysis

### hnscc_pt07_tp53_disruptive_platinum_refractory
- **Subtype:** Disruptive TP53 Mutant Hypopharyngeal Cancer
- **Hospital Treatment:** Carboplatin + Paclitaxel
- **Outcome:** Refractory at 2.8 mo, distant bone metastases
- **Resistance:** Total absence of p53 eliminates transactivation of pro-apoptotic BAX

### hnscc_pt08_pan_refractory_egfr_pi3k_p53_triplet
- **Subtype:** Pan-Refractory Triplet-Hit Head & Neck Cancer
- **Hospital Treatment:** Cetuximab + Chemotherapy followed by IO salvage
- **Outcome:** Rapid failure at 1.7 mo, death at 3.9 mo
- **Resistance:** Simultaneous activation of EGFR and PI3K with p53 loss establishes total resistance
