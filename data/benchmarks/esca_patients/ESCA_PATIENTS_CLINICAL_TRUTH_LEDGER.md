# Official Clinical Ledger: Esophageal Carcinoma (ESCA) Patients

### esca_pt01_her2_amp_trastuzumab_resistant
- **Subtype:** HER2+ Esophageal Adenocarcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Trastuzumab + Cisplatin/5-FU
- **Outcome:** Progression at 4.6 mo, celiac adenopathy
- **Resistance:** EGFR and PI3K/Akt activation bypasses HER2 blockade

### esca_pt02_pembrolizumab_cured_pd_l1_high
- **Subtype:** Esophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma (ESCC)
- **Hospital Treatment:** Pembrolizumab + Chemo (KEYNOTE-590)
- **Outcome:** Complete Remission >42 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Absence of rigid anti-apoptotic defenses facilitates rapid cytotoxic T-cell death

### esca_pt03_ccnd1_amp_cdk4_refractory
- **Subtype:** CCND1-Amplified Advanced ESCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Nivolumab + Ipilimumab (CheckMate-648)
- **Outcome:** Primary refractory, rapid death at 2.4 mo
- **Resistance:** Unrestrained CDK4/6 activity drives relentless S-phase entry

### esca_pt04_pi3k_mutant_platinum_resistant
- **Subtype:** PIK3CA E545K Activated Adenocarcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** FOLFOX chemotherapy
- **Outcome:** Early failure at 3.0 mo, hepatic spread
- **Resistance:** Constitutive Akt flux continuously phosphorylates BAD

### esca_pt05_cdkn2a_null_radiochemo_refractory
- **Subtype:** Biallelic CDKN2A Deleted ESCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Chemoradiotherapy (CROSS trial protocol)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 3.5 mo, tracheoesophageal fistula
- **Resistance:** Loss of p16INK4a hyperactivates CDK4/6, blocking radiation senescence

### esca_pt06_egfr_overexpressed_cetuximab_failure
- **Subtype:** EGFR-Driven Mid-Thoracic ESCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Cetuximab + Paclitaxel
- **Outcome:** Progression at 3.2 mo, bone metastases
- **Resistance:** Parallel PI3K and STAT3 survival transcription sustains Mcl-1

### esca_pt07_tp53_nonsense_fulminant_lethal
- **Subtype:** Disruptive TP53 Null Esophageal Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Docetaxel + Cisplatin + 5-FU
- **Outcome:** Primary chemo-resistance, death at 3.9 mo
- **Resistance:** Inability to transactivate BAX/PUMA upon DNA crosslinks

### esca_pt08_pan_refractory_triplet_hit_lethal
- **Subtype:** Pan-Refractory Triplet-Hit Gastroesophageal Cancer
- **Hospital Treatment:** Trastuzumab Deruxtecan salvage
- **Outcome:** Rapid failure at 1.8 mo, death at 3.5 mo
- **Resistance:** Co-amplification of RTK and cell cycle drivers creates absolute apoptosis lock
