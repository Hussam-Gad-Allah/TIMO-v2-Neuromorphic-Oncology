# Official Clinical Ledger: Gastric & Gastroesophageal Adenocarcinoma (STAD - Stomach) Patients

### stad_pt01_her2_amp_trastuzumab_resistant
- **Subtype:** HER2-Amplified Intestinal Gastric Adenocarcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Trastuzumab + FOLFOX
- **Outcome:** Progression at 5.4 mo, hepatic dissemination
- **Resistance:** MET amplification restores downstream PI3K/Akt flux despite Trastuzumab HER2 blockade

### stad_pt02_msi_high_checkpoint_cured
- **Subtype:** MSI-High / dMMR Hypermutated Gastric Cancer
- **Hospital Treatment:** Nivolumab + Chemo (CheckMate-649)
- **Outcome:** Complete Remission >48 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Absence of hardwired anti-apoptotic locks allows cytotoxic T-cells to eradicate the tumor

### stad_pt03_cdh1_mutant_diffuse_signet_ring
- **Subtype:** Hereditary Diffuse Gastric Carcinoma (HDGC)
- **Hospital Treatment:** ECF (Epirubicin, Cisplatin, 5-FU)
- **Outcome:** Primary chemo-refractory, linitis plastica, death at 4.9 mo
- **Resistance:** Loss of E-cadherin activates Rho/Akt survival axis and suppresses Bax transcription

### stad_pt04_ebv_positive_immune_responsive
- **Subtype:** Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV)-Associated Gastric Cancer
- **Hospital Treatment:** Pembrolizumab monotherapy
- **Outcome:** Durable Partial Response >28 mo
- **Resistance:** High immune antigenicity and low chromosomal instability facilitate apoptotic collapse

### stad_pt05_met_amplified_fulminant_resistant
- **Subtype:** High-Level MET Amplified Gastric Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Capmatinib salvage therapy
- **Outcome:** Progression at 3.0 mo, peritoneal carcinomatosis
- **Resistance:** Rapid feedback upregulation of EGFR and MCL-1 overcomes selective MET TKI

### stad_pt06_pi3k_mutant_platinum_refractory
- **Subtype:** PIK3CA E545K Mutant Gastric Adenocarcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** FLOT chemotherapy (Docetaxel, Oxaliplatin, 5-FU)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 2.8 mo, death at 6.2 mo
- **Resistance:** Constitutive Akt phosphorylation directly inactivates BAD and protects from platinum adducts

### stad_pt07_egfr_amplified_cetuximab_failure
- **Subtype:** EGFR-Driven Proximal Cardia Adenocarcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Cetuximab + Irinotecan
- **Outcome:** Progression at 3.6 mo, retroperitoneal adenopathy
- **Resistance:** Parallel HER2/HER3 heterodimerization maintains downstream MAPK and Mcl-1 flux

### stad_pt08_pan_refractory_her2_met_p53_triplet
- **Subtype:** Pan-Refractory Triplet-Hit Gastric Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Trastuzumab Deruxtecan (T-DXd) salvage
- **Outcome:** Primary refractory, rapid death at 2.2 mo
- **Resistance:** Dual RTK amplification with p53 loss creates an impenetrable anti-apoptotic barrier
