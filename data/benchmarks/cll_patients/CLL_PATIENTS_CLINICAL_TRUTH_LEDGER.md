# Official Clinical Ledger: Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL) Patients

### cll_pt01_del17p_tp53_fludarabine_resistant
- **Subtype:** High-Risk 17p-Deleted / TP53-Mutant CLL
- **Hospital Treatment:** FCR chemoimmunotherapy (Fludarabine, Cyclophosphamide, Rituximab)
- **Outcome:** Primary refractory, rapid lymphocytosis at 2.9 mo, death at 7.1 mo
- **Resistance:** Absence of p53 renders purine analog DNA damage completely futile

### cll_pt02_btk_c481s_ibrutinib_resistant
- **Subtype:** Acquired Ibrutinib-Resistant Relapsed CLL
- **Hospital Treatment:** Ibrutinib (covalent BTK inhibitor) 420mg daily
- **Outcome:** Progression at 4.2 mo, explosive Richter transformation
- **Resistance:** C481S mutation prevents covalent bond formation by Ibrutinib

### cll_pt03_venetoclax_responder_bcl2_hyperdependent
- **Subtype:** Relapsed/Refractory BCL2-Dependent CLL
- **Hospital Treatment:** Venetoclax (ABT-199 selective BCL-2 BH3 mimetic)
- **Outcome:** Undetectable Minimal Residual Disease (uMRD) >40 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Severe mitochondrial dependency on BCL-2 allows instantaneous apoptosis

### cll_pt04_plcg2_mutant_ibrutinib_bypass
- **Subtype:** PLCG2 R665W Mutant Post-BTKi Refractory CLL
- **Hospital Treatment:** Acalabrutinib (2nd-generation BTK inhibitor)
- **Outcome:** Primary failure at 2.4 mo, splenomegaly, death at 5.0 mo
- **Resistance:** Downstream activation of PLCG2 bypasses all BTK inhibitors

### cll_pt05_bcl2_g101v_acquired_venetoclax_resistant
- **Subtype:** Acquired Venetoclax-Resistant Relapsed CLL
- **Hospital Treatment:** Venetoclax salvage therapy
- **Outcome:** Progression at 5.1 mo, massive generalized lymphadenopathy
- **Resistance:** G101V mutation causes steric clash reducing Venetoclax affinity 180-fold

### cll_pt06_notch1_mutated_richter_transformation
- **Subtype:** NOTCH1 P2514fs Mutant Aggressive CLL
- **Hospital Treatment:** R-CHOP chemoimmunotherapy
- **Outcome:** Refractory at 2.1 mo, diffuse large B-cell lymphoma transformation
- **Resistance:** Constitutive NOTCH1/NF-kB activation drives massive MCL-1 survival

### cll_pt07_sf3b1_mutant_spliceosome_aberrant
- **Subtype:** SF3B1 K700E Splicing-Deficient Poor-Prognosis CLL
- **Hospital Treatment:** Bendamustine + Rituximab (BR)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 4.0 mo, marrow failure, death at 8.2 mo
- **Resistance:** Aberrant pre-mRNA splicing insulates pro-survival Bcl-2 family proteins

### cll_pt08_pan_refractory_del17p_btk_bcl2_triplet
- **Subtype:** Pan-Refractory Triplet-Hit Ultra-Lethal CLL
- **Hospital Treatment:** Pirtobrutinib + CAR-T salvage
- **Outcome:** Death at 1.9 mo, fulminant leukemic meningitis
- **Resistance:** Combined evasion of DNA checkpoints, BTK inhibition, and BCL-2 monotherapy
