| **hcc_pt04_tp53_null_aflatoxin_steatohepatitic** | Metabolic Dysfunction / Aflatoxin B1 Steatohepatitic HCC (Pathognomonic TP53 R249S missense mutation, loss of G2/M arrest, high genomic instability) | Transarterial Chemoembolization (TACE with Doxorubicin) followed by Sorafenib | **Primary failure, rapid extrahepatic pulmonary metastases at 3.1 months, death at 6.9 months** | Mutant p53 fails to transactivate BAX and PUMA upon doxorubicin DNA crosslinks, conferring intrinsic chemo-refractoriness |
| **hcc_pt05_axin1_loss_wnt_driven_refractory** | AXIN1-Deficient Hepatocellular Carcinoma (Homozygous AXIN1 loss-of-function frameshift, destruction-complex disruption) | Regorafenib (second-line multikinase inhibitor) | **Progression-free survival of 2.7 months, progressive bile duct compression, death at 6.5 months** | Loss of AXIN1 disrupts the beta-catenin destruction complex independently of CTNNB1, sustaining high Cyclin D1 and Bcl-2 transcription |
| **hcc_pt06_msi_high_atezolizumab_responder** | Microsatellite Instability-High (dMMR / MSI-H) Hepatocellular Carcinoma (Hypermutated phenotype, MLH1 promoter methylation, absence of Wnt/CTNNB1 locks) | Atezolizumab + Bevacizumab | **Durable Complete Response (CR), resolution of primary tumor >36 months (**CURED / Surviving**)** | Intense CD8+ tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte infiltration easily collapses shallow cancer attractors when VEGF/PD-L1 checkpoints are removed |
| **hcc_pt07_pten_null_akt_mcl1_hyperactive** | PTEN-Deficient Aggressive Hepatocellular Carcinoma (Homozygous PTEN deletion, constitutive Akt/mTOR phosphorylation, Mcl-1 overexpression) | Cabozantinib (MET / VEGFR2 / AXL inhibitor) | **Primary resistance, progression at 3.0 months, liver failure, death at 6.1 months** | PTEN loss bypasses all receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitors by feeding directly into PIP3 and AKT, stabilizing MCL-1 and blocking Bax oligomerization |
| **hcc_pt08_pan_refractory_ctnnb1_p53_tert_triplet** | Pan-Refractory Quadruple-Feature Lethal HCC (Concurrent CTNNB1 activating mutation, TP53 null mutation, and TERT promoter hotspot) | Atezolizumab + Bevacizumab followed by Lenvatinib salvage | **Primary pan-resistance, explosive bilobar progression at 1.8 months, death at 3.9 months** | Co-occurrence of beta-catenin activation and p53 inactivation establishes an impenetrable anti-apoptotic lock across both intrinsic and extrinsic death pathways |
