| **gbm_pt01_classical_egfrvIII** | Classical (IDH-wildtype) (EGFRvIII mutation, PTEN deletion, CDKN2A loss, unmethylated MGMT) | Stupp Protocol (Surgical resection + RT + Temozolomide) | **Rapid recurrence at 5.1 months, deceased at 11.2 months (Chemoresistant)** | Unmethylated MGMT neutralized TMZ alkylation; EGFRvIII/AKT maintained continuous anti-apoptotic survival |
| **gbm_pt02_mesenchymal_radiation_relapse** | Mesenchymal (MET high, Hypoxia/Necrosis, TP53 mutation, PTEN loss, CDKN2A loss) | Stupp Protocol + Bevacizumab (anti-VEGF) upon relapse | **Highly refractory invasive relapse, progression at 3.9 months, death at 8.7 months** | MET-driven STAT3 activation and severe hypoxia driving NF-kB and MCL-1/BCL-xL; TP53 mutation blocks apoptosis |
| **gbm_pt03_proneural_pdgfra_driven** | Proneural (IDH-wildtype) (PDGFRA amplification, CDKN2A deletion, TP53 mutation) | Resection + Temozolomide + Radiotherapy | **Early local recurrence at 4.4 months, death at 10.5 months** | PDGFRA hyperactivation driving RAS/MEK/ERK; CDKN2A deletion disables RB1 cell cycle arrest |
| **gbm_pt04_mgmt_methylated_responder** | Proneural / Classical (MGMT promoter methylated (silenced), PTEN intact, TP53 wild-type) | Standard Stupp Protocol (Concomitant TMZ/RT followed by 6 cycles adjuvant TMZ) | **Complete therapeutic response, progression-free survival >36 months (SURVIVED / Remission)** | Silenced MGMT prevents repair of O6-methylguanine lesions; intact p53 triggers cell cycle arrest and apoptosis |
| **gbm_pt05_erlotinib_failure_pten_loss** | Classical (EGFR amplification/EGFRvIII, PTEN loss, unmethylated MGMT) | Erlotinib (EGFR Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitor) + Temozolomide | **Complete resistance to Erlotinib, rapid radiological progression at 2.1 months, death at 5.8 months** | PTEN loss leaves PI3K/Akt constitutively active, decoupling downstream survival from EGFR inhibition |
| **gbm_pt06_hypoxic_stem_pseudopalisade** | Mesenchymal / Necrotic (Severe hypoxia niche, MET activation, PTEN loss, TP53 mutation) | Carmustine (BCNU) wafer implant + adjuvant Temozolomide | **Early regional recurrence with ependymal seeding, deceased at 6.8 months** | Hypoxic induction of MET and NF-kB drives XIAP and MCL-1 expression, insulating cells against alkylating agents |
| **gbm_pt07_cdk4_amplified_escape** | Classical / Proneural (12q14-15 amplicon (CDK4 + MDM2 co-amplification), CDKN2A deletion) | Standard Stupp Protocol (RT + TMZ) | **Fulminant mitotic progression, recurrence at 4.0 months, death at 9.3 months** | Excess MDM2 degrades P53 despite wild-type status; CDK4 hyperactivation irreversibly inactivates RB1 |
| **gbm_pt08_secondary_mesenchymal_transition** | Secondary Mesenchymal (Hypermutated Relapse) (Pan-RTK co-activation (EGFRvIII + MET + PDGFRA), PTEN loss, TP53 mut, CDKN2A loss) | Second-line Lomustine (CCNU) + Regorafenib | **Refractory to all therapies, widespread leptomeningeal progression, death at 4.6 months** | Multi-RTK redundancy coupled with complete loss of tumor suppressors (PTEN, TP53, CDKN2A); massive mitochondrial anti-apoptotic shield (MCL1/BCLxL/XIAP) |
