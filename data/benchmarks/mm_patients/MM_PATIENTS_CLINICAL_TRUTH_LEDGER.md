# Official Clinical Ledger: Multiple Myeloma (MM) Patients

### mm_pt01_t11_14_venetoclax_responder
- **Subtype:** t(11;14) CCND1 Translocated Multiple Myeloma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Venetoclax + Dexamethasone (BELLINI trial protocol)
- **Outcome:** Complete Remission (CR) with negative MRD >38 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Extreme mitochondrial sensitivity to BCL-2 inhibition triggers swift apoptosis

### mm_pt02_del17p_bortezomib_refractory
- **Subtype:** High-Risk del(17p) Proteasome-Inhibitor Refractory MM
- **Hospital Treatment:** Bortezomib + Lenalidomide + Dexamethasone (VRd)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 4.0 mo, extramedullary plasmacytomas, death at 8.1 mo
- **Resistance:** Loss of p53 prevents proteotoxic stress-induced pro-apoptotic signaling

### mm_pt03_t4_14_high_risk_mcl1_dependent
- **Subtype:** t(4;14) FGFR3/MMSET Epigenetic Aggressive MM
- **Hospital Treatment:** Carfilzomib + Pomalidomide + Dexamethasone (KPd)
- **Outcome:** Early failure at 3.2 mo, widespread osteolytic lesions
- **Resistance:** MMSET directly drives sustained MCL-1 transcription, insulating plasma cells

### mm_pt04_daratumumab_cured_cd38_high
- **Subtype:** Standard-Risk Newly Diagnosed Multiple Myeloma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Daratumumab (anti-CD38) + VRd (POLLUX / GRIFFIN)
- **Outcome:** Stringent Complete Response (sCR) >50 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Potent antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity easily collapses myeloma basin

### mm_pt05_kras_g12d_extramedullary_fulminant
- **Subtype:** KRAS-Mutant Extramedullary Myeloma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Elotuzumab + Pomalidomide + Dexamethasone
- **Outcome:** Primary refractory at 2.2 mo, circulating plasma cells, death at 4.6 mo
- **Resistance:** Constitutive MAPK and PI3K signaling bypasses proteasome inhibition

### mm_pt06_myc_rearranged_aggressive_plasmablastic
- **Subtype:** Secondary MYC-Translocated Plasmablastic Myeloma
- **Hospital Treatment:** DCEP chemotherapy (Dexamethasone, Cyclo, Etoposide, Cisplatin)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 2.6 mo, hypercalcemic crisis, death at 5.2 mo
- **Resistance:** Massive MYC overexpression overrides all cell-cycle checkpoints

### mm_pt07_il6_autocrine_bone_marrow_protective
- **Subtype:** Stromal Cytokine-Dependent Myeloma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Ixazomib + Lenalidomide + Dexamethasone
- **Outcome:** Progression at 4.8 mo, diffuse bone marrow replacement
- **Resistance:** IL-6/STAT3 signaling continuously upregulates MCL-1 and BCL-xL

### mm_pt08_pan_refractory_penta_refractory_lethal
- **Subtype:** Penta-Refractory Quadruple-Driver Lethal Myeloma
- **Hospital Treatment:** CAR-T (Cilta-cel) followed by Bispecific T-cell engager salvage
- **Outcome:** Rapid relapse at 1.9 mo, multi-organ failure, death at 3.2 mo
- **Resistance:** Simultaneous disruption of proteostasis, p53, and MAPK produces absolute pan-resistance
