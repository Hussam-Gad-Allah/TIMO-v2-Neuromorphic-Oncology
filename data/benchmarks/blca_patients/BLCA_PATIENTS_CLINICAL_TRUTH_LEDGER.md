# Official Clinical Ledger: Bladder & Urothelial Carcinoma (BLCA) Patients

### blca_pt01_fgfr3_s249c_erdafitinib_resistant
- **Subtype:** Luminal-Papillary FGFR3-Mutant Bladder Cancer
- **Hospital Treatment:** Erdafitinib (selective oral FGFR1-4 inhibitor)
- **Outcome:** PFS 5.5 mo, rapid bilateral pelvic progression
- **Resistance:** Parallel PIK3CA activation and MCL-1 stabilization bypasses FGFR3 inhibition

### blca_pt02_her2_amplified_platinum_failure
- **Subtype:** ERBB2-Amplified Infiltrating Urothelial Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Gemcitabine + Cisplatin
- **Outcome:** Primary chemo-refractory at 3.0 mo, retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy
- **Resistance:** HER2 hyperactivation insulates cancer cells against platinum crosslinks

### blca_pt03_tmb_high_pembrolizumab_cured
- **Subtype:** Basal-Squamous High-TMB Immunotherapy Responsive
- **Hospital Treatment:** Pembrolizumab (anti-PD-1)
- **Outcome:** Complete Remission >45 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Absence of rigid anti-apoptotic defenses facilitates rapid cytotoxic T-cell lysis

### blca_pt04_rb1_p53_double_null_neuroendocrine
- **Subtype:** Small-Cell / Neuroendocrine Transformed BLCA
- **Hospital Treatment:** Etoposide + Cisplatin
- **Outcome:** Transient response 3.2 mo, rapid visceral death at 6.0 mo
- **Resistance:** Bypass of all cell-cycle checkpoints confers aggressive apoptosis resistance

### blca_pt05_kdm6a_mutant_epigenetic_instability
- **Subtype:** KDM6A Epigenetic Altered Urothelial Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Enfortumab Vedotin + Pembrolizumab (EV-302)
- **Outcome:** PFS 24.1 mo, outstanding clinical benefit
- **Resistance:** High epigenetic replication stress sensitizes tumor to combined targeted perturbation

### blca_pt06_pi3k_mutant_gemcitabine_resistant
- **Subtype:** PIK3CA E542K Activated Infiltrating Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Gemcitabine + Carboplatin
- **Outcome:** Early progression at 2.9 mo, lung metastases
- **Resistance:** Constitutive AKT flux phosphorylates BAD and suppresses mitochondrial death

### blca_pt07_fgfr3_fusion_acquired_gatekeeper_mut
- **Subtype:** FGFR3-TACC3 Translocated Advanced Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Infigratinib salvage
- **Outcome:** Progression at 3.4 mo, bony metastases
- **Resistance:** Gatekeeper mutation produces steric clash in ATP pocket

### blca_pt08_pan_refractory_fgfr_her2_p53_triplet
- **Subtype:** Pan-Refractory Triplet-Hit Urothelial Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Chemo-IO followed by ADC salvage
- **Outcome:** Rapid failure at 1.9 mo, liver failure
- **Resistance:** Dual RTK hyperactivation with p53 loss locks the apoptotic threshold
