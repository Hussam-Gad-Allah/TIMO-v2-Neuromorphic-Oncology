| **skcm_pt01_braf_v600e_vemurafenib_resistant** | Cutaneous Melanoma (BRAF-Mutant Subtype) (BRAF V600E activating mutation with secondary NRAS Q61K acquired bypass) | Vemurafenib (selective oral BRAF inhibitor) | **Initial tumor shrinkage for 5.1 months, then explosive subcutaneous and lung metastases, death at 10.2 months** | Acquired NRAS mutation switches signaling through CRAF homodimers and hyperactivates PI3K, restoring ERK flux despite Vemurafenib |
| **skcm_pt02_nras_q61r_hyperactivated_refractory** | NRAS-Mutant Cutaneous Melanoma (Non-V600) (NRAS Q61R GTPase-deficient mutation, wild-type BRAF, high CRAF expression) | Binimetinib (MEK162 selective MEK inhibitor) | **Transient disease stabilization for 2.8 months, rapid axillary nodal progression and liver lesions, death at 7.4 months** | MEK inhibition induces rapid feedback release of CRAF and hyperactivates PI3K/Akt, rescuing MITF and Cyclin D1 |
| **skcm_pt03_braf_pten_double_null_fulminant** | BRAF-Mutant / PTEN-Loss Aggressive Melanoma (BRAF V600E mutation, homozygous PTEN deletion, constitutive Akt phosphorylation) | Dabrafenib + Trametinib (BRAF + MEK combination therapy) | **Primary resistance with disease progression at 3.2 months, multiple brain metastases, death at 6.8 months** | PTEN deletion maintains unchecked PI3K/Akt signaling, continuously stabilizing Mcl-1 and inactivating BAD despite complete MAPK blockade |
| **skcm_pt04_braf_wt_checkpoint_exceptional_responder** | Triple-Wild-Type Cutaneous Melanoma (High UV Mutational Load) (Extensive UV-induced C>T transitions, wild-type BRAF/NRAS/cKIT, intact p53 axis) | Nivolumab (anti-PD-1) + Ipilimumab (anti-CTLA-4) | **Complete pathological and radiological response (CR), ongoing disease-free survival >60 months (SURVIVED / Cured)** | Intact intrinsic apoptotic machinery; absence of oncogene-driven Bcl-2/Mcl-1 locks permits rapid cytotoxic lymphocyte granzyme-mediated death |
| **skcm_pt05_ckit_l576p_acral_mucosal_resistant** | Acral Lentiginous Melanoma (Non-Sun-Exposed Subtype) (c-KIT exon 11 L576P activating point mutation, high autocrine HGF/c-MET stromal secretion) | Imatinib mesylate (KIT receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor) | **Progression-free survival of 3.6 months, bulky groin lymphadenopathy and visceral metastases, death at 8.9 months** | Secondary autocrine HGF secretion activates parallel RTK signaling and drives sustained STAT3/Mcl-1 survival transcription |
| **skcm_pt06_nf1_null_desmoplastic_hypermutant** | NF1-Mutant Desmoplastic Melanoma (Biallelic loss-of-function NF1 nonsense mutation, loss of Neurofibromin GTPase-activating function) | Carboplatin + Paclitaxel | **Primary chemo-refractory, perineural invasion along cranial nerves, death at 6.2 months** | NF1 loss hyper-stabilizes wild-type RAS-GTP loading, continuously feeding both MAPK and PI3K/Akt survival loops |
| **skcm_pt07_mitf_amplified_stem_mesenchymal** | MITF-Amplified Melanoma with Stem-Like Features (High-level focal MITF genomic amplification, concurrent Wnt/beta-catenin nuclear flux) | Dabrafenib + Trametinib salvage therapy | **Rapid recurrence at 3.4 months, widespread cutaneous in-transit and hepatic metastases, death at 7.9 months** | MITF amplification directly drives massive Bcl-2 and Cyclin D1 transcription, establishing an impenetrable anti-apoptotic lock |
| **skcm_pt08_pan_refractory_quadruple_pathway_metastasis** | Pan-Refractory Quadruple-Hit Metastatic Melanoma (Concurrent BRAF V600K, PTEN loss, CDKN2A deletion, TP53 mut, high autocrine HGF) | Ipilimumab + Nivolumab followed by Dabrafenib + Trametinib | **Progression-free survival of 1.7 months, disseminated leptomeningeal and splenic metastases, death at 4.1 months** | Simultaneous disruption of MAPK, PI3K, p53, and cell cycle checkpoints induces constitutive MCL-1, BCL-xL, and XIAP expression |
