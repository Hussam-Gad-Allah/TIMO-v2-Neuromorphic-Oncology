# Official Clinical Ledger: Clear Cell Renal Cell Carcinoma (ccRCC - Kidney) Patients

### ccrcc_pt01_vhl_null_sunitinib_resistant
- **Subtype:** VHL-Null / HIF2A Driven ccRCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Sunitinib monotherapy
- **Outcome:** Progression at 4.2 mo, lung metastases
- **Resistance:** Secondary AXL/MET upregulation restores AKT flux despite VEGFR inhibition

### ccrcc_pt02_pbrm1_loss_nivo_responder
- **Subtype:** PBRM1-Deficient Immunotherapy Responsive ccRCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Nivolumab (anti-PD-1)
- **Outcome:** Durable Complete Response >40 mo (CURED)
- **Resistance:** Absence of rigid anti-apoptotic defenses permits rapid cytotoxic T-cell granzyme lysis

### ccrcc_pt03_bap1_mutant_fulminant_lethal
- **Subtype:** BAP1-Mutant High-Grade Lethal ccRCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Cabozantinib + Nivolumab
- **Outcome:** Primary refractory, rapid progression at 2.6 mo
- **Resistance:** BAP1 loss drives massive metabolic rewiring and constitutive MCL-1 translation

### ccrcc_pt04_pten_null_temsirolimus_failure
- **Subtype:** PI3K/PTEN-Loss Refractory Renal Carcinoma
- **Hospital Treatment:** Temsirolimus (mTORC1 inhibitor)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 3.1 mo, hepatic metastases
- **Resistance:** mTORC1 inhibition unleashes feedback hyperactivation of AKT and MCL-1

### ccrcc_pt05_setd2_mutant_epigenetic_instability
- **Subtype:** SETD2 Histone Methyltransferase Deficient ccRCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Lenvatinib + Pembrolizumab
- **Outcome:** PFS 18.2 mo, significant clinical benefit
- **Resistance:** High epigenetic replication stress sensitizes tumor to multi-targeted kinase uncoupling

### ccrcc_pt06_hif2a_belzutifan_resistant_bypass
- **Subtype:** HIF-2a Antagonist Resistant ccRCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Belzutifan (HIF-2a selective inhibitor)
- **Outcome:** Progression at 5.0 mo, retroperitoneal recurrence
- **Resistance:** Pocket mutation prevents Belzutifan binding, maintaining VEGF transcription

### ccrcc_pt07_sarcomatoid_dedifferentiated_aggressive
- **Subtype:** Sarcomatoid Transformed ccRCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Ipilimumab + Nivolumab
- **Outcome:** Early failure at 2.0 mo, brain metastases
- **Resistance:** Meszenchymal transition establishes high XIAP and BCL-xL survival buffer

### ccrcc_pt08_pan_refractory_triplet_hit_lethal
- **Subtype:** Pan-Refractory Quadruple Alteration ccRCC
- **Hospital Treatment:** Lenvatinib + Everolimus salvage
- **Outcome:** Death at 3.5 mo, widespread dissemination
- **Resistance:** Combined loss of VHL, BAP1, and PTEN confers total resistance to all TKI regimens
